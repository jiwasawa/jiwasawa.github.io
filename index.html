Junichiro Iwasawa

岩澤 諄一郎

I am a Solutions Architect at NVIDIA (since February 2026), working with Japanese enterprise customers on large language model training and inference, with a focus on the NeMo framework.

Previously, I was a Researcher and Tech Lead at Preferred Networks Inc. (2021–2026), where I led BtoB machine learning solutions in healthcare and life sciences—including open medical LLMs surpassing GPT-4 on the Japanese Medical Licensing Exam and AI-based MRI diagnostic support—and mentored four research interns and one part-time engineer.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics from the University of Tokyo (Furusawa Laboratory), studying evolutionary constraints and antibiotic resistance in E. coli with machine learning, and my M.S. in Physics (Sano Laboratory), studying active matter and collective motion of Janus colloidal particles.
